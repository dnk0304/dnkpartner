## 💰 Cost Optimization Tips

### For Chat/Text:
1. **Use GPT-5 Nano** for bulk prompt generation ($0.05/1M input vs $2.50/1M for GPT-4o)
2. **Use GPT-5** for balanced quality/cost
3. **Reserve GPT-5.2** for complex creative tasks only

### For Images:
1. **Gemini 3 Pro** is the most cost-effective (~16x cheaper than DALL-E 3)
2. Use **1K resolution** for drafts, **4K** only for finals
3. Batch multiple prompts to reduce overhead

### For Videos:
1. **Veo 3** offers best value for standard quality
2. Keep videos **5-10 seconds** for cost efficiency
3. Use lower quality for concept testing
