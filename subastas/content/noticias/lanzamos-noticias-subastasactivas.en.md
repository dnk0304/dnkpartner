Today we are launching **News**, the new current-affairs section of
SubastasActivas.

## What you will find here

Until now, our editorial content lived in the [guides](/blog): practical,
evergreen material on how Spanish judicial (BOE) auctions work. News covers
the opposite — what changes:

- **Regulatory changes** affecting judicial auctions in Spain.
- **Market data**: auction volumes, the most active provinces and trends we
  detect in the official Auction Portal data.
- **SubastasActivas updates**: new features, improvements and service
  notices.

## Why a separate section

Guides answer "how does this work?"; news answers "what happened this week?".
Keeping them separate keeps both useful: guides do not expire, and news always
shows its publication date.

## How to follow along

Every post appears in this section with its date. If you want alerts for
specific auctions, you can [create an alert](/en/alerts) with your province,
type and price filters.

Thanks for being here. This is just the beginning.
